rcloner

rcloner is a personal backup tool. It uses rclone to copy the owner's own files to the owner's own Google Drive account.

It is a single-user application and is not offered to the public.

Privacy

rcloner does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. It does not operate any server that receives user data.

The application accesses Google Drive only after the owner signs in with Google and grants permission. Access is used solely to upload and manage the owner's own backup files in the owner's own Drive.

Authorization tokens are stored locally on the owner's own machine. No data is transmitted to the developer or to any third party other than Google Drive, as directed by the owner.

The owner can revoke access at any time from their Google Account permissions page.
